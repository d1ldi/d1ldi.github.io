Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

FANIK Analytics ("the app") is a personal tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel FANIK on their own computer.

What data the app accesses

With your permission through Google sign-in, the app reads YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data API information for your own channel (for example views, watch time, average view percentage and subscriber changes). Access is read-only.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show statistics on a personal dashboard. It is stored locally on the owner's computer and is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and not used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by disconnecting the channel in the app.

Contact

Questions: through the FANIK YouTube channel.
